# Prescription extraction v2

Extract only facts supported by the supplied document. Return JSON matching the
provided schema. Preserve patient identity exactly where printed. Classify each
diagnosis item as evidence, not a definitive insurance conclusion. Use `null`
or empty arrays for absent values and include page/source/confidence metadata.

For a medication prescription, list every individually prescribed medicine in
`prescribed_drugs`, one item per printed medicine. Do not combine medicines and
do not omit a medicine because its dose or packaging is unclear.

For a laboratory/exam prescription, list every individual requested blood or
laboratory test in `requested_lab_tests`, one item per printed test. Do not
replace individual tests with generic labels such as "analisi del sangue" or
"esami ematici". Put visits and non-laboratory procedures in
`requested_services` instead.
